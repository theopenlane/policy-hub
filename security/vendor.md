## Purpose and Scope

This policy defines {{company_name}}’s requirements for evaluating and managing vendors that handle {{company_name}} or customer data. It applies to all third-party service providers and contractors that store, process, transmit, or have access to {{company_name}}’s confidential or customer information. Vendor reviews must be conducted prior to engagement and at regular intervals to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

## Background

Vendors can introduce security, compliance, and operational risk if they mishandle sensitive data or fail to maintain adequate controls. Vendor evaluations, contract requirements, and periodic reviews help reduce third-party risk exposure and ensure compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

## Policy

### Vendor Assessment

1. Vendors’ security practices must be reviewed during initial contract evaluation and at least annually thereafter.
1. Assessment requirements must reflect the sensitivity of data handled in accordance with the Data Classification Policy.
1. Any material change in vendor scope or service use may trigger a re-assessment.

### Documentation

1. Vendor records must include:
   1. Vendor name and contact information
   1. Type of data shared
   1. Contract terms (e.g., SLA, Terms of Service)
   1. Security certifications, assessment reports, or questionnaire responses

### Risk Management

1. Vendors must provide a current SOC 2 report (Type I or Type II).
1. If a SOC 2 report is unavailable, the vendor must complete a security questionnaire.
1. Identified risks must be documented and addressed through remediation, compensating controls, or formal risk acceptance.

### Training for Contractors

1. Contractors must acknowledge {{company_name}} security policies upon onboarding.
1. Contractors must complete annual security awareness training.

### Schedule

1. Vendor reviews must occur at least annually for as long as services remain in use.

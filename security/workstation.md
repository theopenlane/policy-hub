## Purpose and Scope

This policy defines requirements for securing workstations to reduce the risk of data loss, theft, or unauthorized access. It applies to all employees and contractors who use company-owned or personal devices to access {{company_name}} systems or data. For the purposes of this policy, a *workstation* is any device that stores or accesses {{company_name}} information.

## Policy

### General Requirements

1. Workstations must meet the following criteria:
   1. The operating system must be no more than one major version behind the current stable release.
   1. The device must be encrypted at rest.
   1. The device must be locked when not in use or when unattended.
   1. Workstations must be used for authorized business purposes only.
   1. Lost, stolen, or damaged devices must be reported immediately.

### Desktop & Laptop Devices

1. Employees will be issued a desktop, laptop, or both, based on job requirements. Contractors must use their own laptops unless otherwise authorized.
1. Desktops and laptops must operate on macOS or Windows.

### Mobile Devices

1. Mobile devices must comply with the Removable Media Policy, Cloud Storage Policy, and Bring Your Own Device Policy.
1. Mobile devices must operate on iOS or Android.
1. Company data may only be accessed using approved and secured applications.

### Removable Media

1. Removable media must comply with the Removable Media Policy, Cloud Storage Policy, and Bring Your Own Device Policy.
1. Removable media may be used only on approved devices and only when its use does not conflict with other security policies.

## Purpose and Scope

This policy defines {{company_name}}’s requirements for evaluating and supporting personnel to reduce security risk and ensure appropriate safeguards are in place. It applies to all employees, contractors, and others with access to {{company_name}} systems or data.

## Background

People are a critical part of {{company_name}}’s security posture. Proper screening, training, and evaluation help reduce the risk of insider threats, accidental data exposure, or security misconduct. These practices also ensure personnel remain aligned with organizational expectations, legal requirements, and industry security frameworks.

## Policy

### Reference Checks

1. Criminal background checks are not performed.
1. Reference checks must be completed for all prospective hires either prior to their start date or within 30 days of hire.
1. Results of reference checks must be documented and stored in the employee’s personnel file.

### Security Awareness Training

1. All employees must complete information security awareness training during onboarding.
1. Annual security awareness training is required for all personnel with continued access to {{company_name}} systems and data.

### Performance Reviews

1. All full-time employees must participate in an annual performance review.
1. Performance reviews must be signed and dated by both the employee and their manager.
1. Completed reviews must be stored in the employee’s personnel file within the HR system.
